CAFFELINE

Your cozy neighborhood cat café where artisanal coffee meets feline comfort.

Welcome to Caffeline

Every cup is purrfectly brewed and served alongside delicious pastries in a warm, relaxing atmosphere. Whether you’re catching up on work, meeting with friends, or simply looking to unwind with the soothing company of our friendly resident cats, Caffeline is your ultimate hideaway to relax, cuddle, and recharge.

Our Story

What started as a simple dream over a quiet morning cup of coffee quickly turned into a sanctuary for both cat lovers and caffeine enthusiasts alike. We opened Caffeline to create a warm, homey space where rescue cats could thrive in a loving environment while guests enjoy handcrafted beverages and fresh pastries. Every corner of our café is designed to bring a little extra comfort, warmth, and purrs to your everyday routine.
